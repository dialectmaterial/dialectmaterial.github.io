## Absolute knowing {#absolute-knowing}

The goal of Hegel's *Phenomenology of Spirit*: Spirit that knows itself as Spirit. It is not a final edification where one can stop working through one's own shattering. It inaugurates the process in which Spirit deals with negativity on a higher order, which is proper philosophizing.

*In:* [The "I" as a Funny Circle](/articles/the-i-as-a-funny-circle/), [Spiritual Practice](/articles/spiritual-practice-the-forward-return/), [Who Can Philosophize?](/articles/who-can-philosophize/)

## Big Other {#big-other}

Lacan's name for the symbolic order that seems to guarantee that reality makes sense. The point is that it is internally incoherent with itself, not a guarantee of meaning.

*In:* [Who Can Philosophize?](/articles/who-can-philosophize/), [The Refusal to Enjoy](/articles/the-refusal-to-enjoy/), [Introduction to The Parallax View](/articles/the-parallax-view/)

## Cogito {#cogito}

Descartes's *I think, therefore I am*, the hard rock that universal doubt runs into. One could just as well say: I doubt, therefore I think, therefore I am. It is a doubt in which one's identity is at stake, a point of knowing one's own unknowing.

*In:* [The Refusal to Enjoy](/articles/the-refusal-to-enjoy/), [To Live Philosophically](/articles/to-live-philosophically/)

## Communisation {#communisation}

A notion from the crises of the late 1960s and early 1970s, born in the left-communist critique of the communist movement. Communism is then not a stage reached through the dictatorship of the proletariat and socialism; communisation already exists as the real movement of the liberation of value. Here it names an effort to bring the inner logic of capital, the infinitisation of bad infinity, to an end.

*In:* [Communisation in Absolute Capitalism](/articles/communisation-in-absolute-capitalism/)

## Concept {#concept}

The foremost concept of Hegel's *Science of Logic*, to which the whole Doctrine of the Concept is devoted. It is not something we *have*, as in "I have the concept of a thing". It is the movement of the "I" itself, which Kant could not hold fast to.

*In:* [The "I" as a Funny Circle](/articles/the-i-as-a-funny-circle/), [The Dialectic of the 'I'](/articles/the-dialectic-of-the-i/)

## Death drive {#death-drive}

Freud's name for the compulsion to repeat. Not the sublime immortality of a heavenly afterlife, but the undead, indestructible drive in this life. In psychoanalysis, self-related negativity is radicalised into the death drive.

*In:* [Everyone Knows Perfection Is Absurd](/articles/everyone-knows-perfection-is-absurd/), [Trauma](/articles/trauma/), [Communisation in Absolute Capitalism](/articles/communisation-in-absolute-capitalism/)

## Disavowal {#disavowal}

A form of repression that does not deny the trauma and its consequences. Its formula: "I know that I am traumatized, but because I accept this, I do not need to work on it." It is the "reasonable" form of repression, conscious of traumas and symptoms, which keeps a fact while taking away its weight.

*In:* [Trauma](/articles/trauma/)

## Negativity {#negativity}

The force in Hegel by which every shape of Spirit undoes itself. The moment negativity sets in is a moment of encounter with the truth of the self. Spiritual practice means embracing it, because it is necessary for Spirit's self-knowing.

*In:* [Spiritual Practice](/articles/spiritual-practice-the-forward-return/), [The Mystical Is Rational](/articles/the-mystical-is-rational/)

## Parallax {#parallax}

Ordinarily, an *apparent* shift of an object caused by a change in the observer's position. Žižek drops the word "apparent": the shift in perspective shifts the objective phenomena themselves. The parallax gap is the antagonism between mutually exclusive outlooks with no neutral common ground, and for Žižek the key to dialectical materialism.

*In:* [Introduction to The Parallax View](/articles/the-parallax-view/)

## Short circuit {#short-circuit}

Žižek's image for critical reading, from the foreword to his *Short Circuits* series: to cross wires that do not usually touch, reading a major classic through a "minor" author or concept. Marx short-circuits philosophy and religion with political economy; Freud short-circuits morality with libidinal economy.

*In:* [The Odd One In the Voice and Nothing More](/articles/the-odd-one-in-the-voice-and-nothing-more/)

## Substance {#substance}

For Descartes there are two substances, mind and extension. Spinoza reduces them to one, the underlying unity of reality, God or nature; mind and extension become its attributes.

*In:* [Everyone Knows Perfection Is Absurd](/articles/everyone-knows-perfection-is-absurd/), [The Refusal to Enjoy](/articles/the-refusal-to-enjoy/)
