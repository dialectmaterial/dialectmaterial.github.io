I'm Dimitri F. Crooijmans. I write *Actual Spirit*, a place for philosophy and what it has to say about itself, science and politics today. I live in Leiden, where I study computational linguistics at Leiden University.

## What I work on

Most of my essays start from Hegel: the dialectic of the "I", Spirit, and the claim that the mystical is rational. From there they move to Spinoza and Descartes, to Lacanian psychoanalysis, and to the Slovenian School of Žižek, Zupančič and Dolar. I write about capital and communism, trauma and enjoyment, art and the question of who can philosophize. Some pieces are stories. I write in English and Dutch.

I went to several high schools and studied software engineering at Codam for two years. In 2020, during the pandemic, Žižek got me reading Hegel seriously, and I haven't stopped since.

## Why "Actual Spirit"

<span class="check">The name comes from Hegel. For Hegel, Spirit is not a ghost behind the world. It is what actually happens when people think, speak and live together, and it is actual in the strong sense: it works, it has effects. In the preface to the *Philosophy of Right* he writes that what is rational is actual, and what is actual is rational, and he asks us to recognise reason as the rose in the cross of the present. That rose is the purple rose you see on my channel.</span>

## Lectures and conversations

On my YouTube channel, *Actual Spirit*, I give lectures and hold conversations on philosophy, art, science and politics, in English and Dutch. A good place to start is my [introduction to Žižek's *The Parallax View*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cv43D6GqJds).

At [Philosophy Portal](https://philosophyportal.online) I led the Slovenian School Reading Group, where we read Dolar's *A Voice and Nothing More* and Zupančič's *The Odd One In* together. I've also spoken at its conferences on Nietzsche's *Zarathustra*, Hegel's *Science of Logic*, Lacan's *Écrits* and the Rosy Cross. All of it is on the [Talks](/talks/) page.
